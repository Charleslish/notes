A Date holds one day of the Gregorian calendar as a year, a month, and a day. 
Its invariant is that year_ lies between kMinYear and kMaxYear, month_ lies between 1 and kMonthsPerYear, 
and day_ lies between 1 and the number of days in that month. 
Every range includes both of its ends. The member operators ++, --, +=, and -= move a date by days across 
the ends of months and years, February 29 of a leap year included. Each of them changes day_, 
and the private member function Normalize then restores the invariant. 
A date never moves past either end of the range: a move that would pass December 31 of kMaxYear, 
the last date, stops there, and a move that would pass January 1 of kMinYear, the first date, stops there. 
The constructor keeps the invariant by limiting each argument to its range.

You write the definition of each member function of Date in date.cc.

Private member functions
Signature	Description
bool IsLeapYear() const;	
    Returns true when year_ is a leap year of the Gregorian calendar, and false otherwise. 
    A leap year is a multiple of 400, or a multiple of 4 that is not a multiple of 100, 
    so 2024 and 2000 are leap years, and 2023 and 1900 are not.
int DaysInMonth() const;	
    Returns the number of days in month month_ of year year_. January, March, May, July, August, October, 
    and December have 31 days, and April, June, September, and November have 30. 
    February has 29 days when IsLeapYear() returns true and 28 otherwise. 
    It calls the private member function IsLeapYear to decide the length of February.
int LimitStep(int days) const;	
    Returns days limited to the range from -kMaxStep to kMaxStep: a value below -kMaxStep becomes -kMaxStep, 
    a value above kMaxStep becomes kMaxStep, and every other value is returned unchanged. 
    No date lies more than kMaxStep days from either end of the range, 
    so the limited step ends at the same date as the original one, 
    and adding it to day_ cannot exceed the range of int.
void Normalize();	
    Restores the invariant after day_ has moved outside the days of month_. 
    It is called with year_ and month_ in their ranges and with day_ between 1 - kMaxStep and 31 + kMaxStep. 
    While day_ is greater than DaysInMonth(), it subtracts DaysInMonth() from day_ and moves to the next month, 
    where the month after December is January of the next year. 
    While day_ is less than 1, it moves to the previous month and adds that month's DaysInMonth() to day_, 
    where the month before January is December of the previous year. Then, when year_ is above kMaxYear, 
    the date becomes the last date, and when year_ is below kMinYear, the date becomes the first date. 
    It calls the private member function DaysInMonth for the length of each month.
