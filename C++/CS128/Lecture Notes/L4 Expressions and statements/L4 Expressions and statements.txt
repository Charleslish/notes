L4 Expressions and statements
    && || !
    Precedence: !, (unary -)	logical negation, negation
                *, /, %	multiplication, division, remainder
                +, (binary -)	addition, subtraction
                <<	stream insertion
                <, <=, >, >=	relational comparison
                ==, !=	equality comparison
                &&	logical and (conjunction)
                ||	logical or (disjunction)
                =	assignment
    Associativity:
        the arithmetic and relational operators are left-associative
        Assignment is right-associative
            int x = 0;
            int y = 1;
            x = y = 5;// 5 5 instead of 1 5
    Grouping and order of evaluation: 
        && evaluates its left operand first, and it evaluates its right operand only when the left operand produces true
    Statements
        An expression becomes a statement when a semicolon follows it, and the result is an expression statement.
        A compound statement is a sequence of statements enclosed in braces {}
        A semicolon on its own is a null statement (that does nothing)
